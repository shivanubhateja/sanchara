Common questions

Is Sanchara a hospital or a clinic?

No. Sanchara is a care coordination service. Surgery and medical care are provided by independent accredited hospitals and their surgeons. We do not give medical advice or guarantee outcomes.

How are hospitals chosen?

We refer patients only to hospitals with international accreditation such as JCI or NABH, and to surgeons whose experience we have reviewed.

Will my health insurance cover it?

Standard domestic plans usually do not cover elective treatment abroad. Some international private plans do. Tell us your insurer and we will help you check before you book.

What if there is a complication after I return?

Your discharge records and rehab plan go to your own doctor. We recommend travel and medical-complication insurance and will explain your options before you travel.

Do I need a visa?

Most nationalities need a visa to travel to India for treatment. We guide you through the medical visa process and the documents the hospital provides.

How is my health data protected?

Share records only through the secure channel we give you. We handle data in line with applicable privacy law, including GDPR for EU and UK patients.
